Our Mission

Simplify PC building for everyone in Nepal

Nepal's tech market has grown fast but the tools to support PC builders haven't kept up. Finding compatible parts meant hopping between five different stores, WhatsApp groups, and Facebook posts from 2019. JesterPCs was built to fix that — a single platform where you can browse parts, check compatibility, compare builds, and order everything you need without the confusion.

Whether you're a first-time builder picking up a budget rig or a veteran chasing the best 4K performance money can buy, JesterPCs is built to meet you where you are.

The Story

Why JesterPCs exists

It started with a frustrating afternoon trying to find a compatible GPU for an AM5 build in Kathmandu. The parts existed in Nepal — but finding them, verifying compatibility, and comparing prices meant hours of work across physical stores, DMs, and outdated Facebook groups.

JesterPCs was built as the platform that should have existed. A centralized, organized, and genuinely helpful place to buy PC hardware in Nepal — with real specs, real prices, and real support from people who actually build.

The name? PCs should be fun. Building a custom rig is one of the most satisfying things you can do as a tech enthusiast — and we want the experience of buying the parts to feel the same way.
